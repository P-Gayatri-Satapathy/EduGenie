# AI Modules

EduGenie consists of five AI-powered educational modules.

---

## 1. Question Answering

Answers educational questions accurately using Google Gemini AI.

---

## 2. Concept Explanation

Explains difficult topics in simple language suitable for students.

---

## 3. Quiz Generation

Generates multiple-choice questions with four options and correct answers.

---

## 4. Text Summarization

Converts lengthy educational content into concise bullet-point summaries.

---

## 5. Learning Recommendations

Creates structured learning roadmaps with beginner, intermediate, and advanced resources.

---
