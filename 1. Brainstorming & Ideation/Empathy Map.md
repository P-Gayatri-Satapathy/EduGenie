# Empathy Map

## User

Students, Self-Learners, and Educators

---

## Says

- I don't understand this topic.
- Can you explain it simply?
- I need practice questions.
- I have long notes to study.
- I want a proper learning roadmap.

---

## Thinks

- Learning should be easier.
- I need reliable educational support.
- I want quick answers.
- I should prepare better for exams.

---

## Does

- Searches Google
- Watches YouTube tutorials
- Reads online articles
- Solves quizzes
- Takes notes

---

## Feels

- Confused
- Curious
- Motivated
- Stressed before exams
- Happy when concepts become clear

---

## Pain Points

- Too many learning resources
- Difficult explanations
- Time-consuming revision
- Lack of personalized learning

---

## Goals

- Understand concepts quickly
- Prepare effectively for exams
- Save study time
- Learn independently
- Improve academic performance
